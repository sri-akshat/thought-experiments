Module 1: The Missing 2ab & The Square-Root Trap

Why (a ± b)2 ALWAYS has a middle term, and why √(a2 + b2) can never split into a + b.

Fixes Q3(ii)(b), Q5(iii), Q7(ii) · +9 Marks
What Happened on Term-1 Paper
• In Q7(ii), (6 + 2x)2 became 36 + 4x2 (the 24x middle term vanished), and in Q3(ii)(b), (4 − k)2 became 16 + k2.
• In Q3(ii)(b), 10 = √(k2 + 80) became 10 = k + 4√5.
The Physical Intuition
• Area of a Square: Increasing a side from a to (a + b) adds two wing rectangles of area ab each, not just a tiny b2 corner!
• Hypotenuse Shortcut: √(a2 + b2) is the straight-line diagonal of a right triangle — always shorter than walking a + b along the two legs!

Module 2: “X-Ray Vision” for Factorisation, Cubes & Surds

Spot big algebraic chunks hidden inside long expressions instead of expanding everything blindly.

Fixes Q2(iii), Q5(i), Q5(ii), Q7(iii), Q8(ii) · +14 Marks
What Happened on Term-1 Paper
• Left (x2 + y2 − z2)2 − 4x2y2 and (x + 4)2 − 5xy − 20y − 6y2 blank.
• Wrote a3 + b3 = (a+b)(a2 + b2 − 2ab) (extra 2!), and wrote (5 − 2√6) + (5 + 2√6) = 10 + 4√6.
The X-Ray Habit
• Never expand a bracket that's already squared! Look for Chunk A2 − Chunk B2 or a repeated Chunk U.
• SOAP Rule for Cubes: a3 ± b3 = (a ± b)(a2 ∓ ab + b2) — Same sign, Opposite sign, Always Plus (and single ab, never 2ab!).

Module 4: Assumed Mean Balance Beam, Semi-Annual CI & Logs

Why the Assumed Mean A must be added back, how half-yearly hops work, and finishing logs cleanly.

Fixes Q7(i), Q1(viii), Q2(ii), Q2(i), Q4(ii) · +9 Marks
What Happened on Term-1 Paper
• Q7(i) Assumed Mean: Computed Σfidi = −880 and Σfi = 100 flawlessly, then boxed Mean = −8.8 for weekly wages of ₹800–₹1000!
• Q1(viii): Used annual formula for semi-annual compounding; Q2(i) & Q4(ii): Stopped 1 step before evaluating the final log.
The 2-Second Sanity Check
• Mean = Anchor + Offset: −8.8 is just how far below your temporary anchor A = 900 the true balance point sits: 900 + (−8.8) = ₹891.20!
• Semi-Annual = Every 6 Months: In 1½ years there are 3 six-month hops, each at half the annual rate (R/2 %).

Unlock hidden isosceles triangles with equal-side tick marks, check co-interior angles, and tame 1/x systems.

What Happened on Term-1 Paper
• Q6(iii) Rhombus PQRS + Equilateral ▵RST: Missed that RQ = RS and RT = RS makes RQ = RT (▵RQT is isosceles!).
• Q1(xiii): Divided 140° by 2 (70°) instead of co-interior 180° − 140° = 40°; Q8(i): Skipped u = 1/x, v = 1/y.
The Geometry Habit
• Draw Tick Marks FIRST: Whenever a question says “Rhombus”, “Square”, or “Equilateral”, put identical tick marks on every equal side — hidden isosceles triangles jump off the page!
• Parallel Lines C-Pattern: Co-interior angles inside a ‘C’ add to 180°, never half!
